Strategic Mineral Policy Framework

Punjab Pink Rock Salt Value-Addition & Industrial Investment Framework

The Directorate General of Mines & Minerals facilitates the structural transformation of Pakistan's pink salt sector—moving from low-yield raw rock extraction to modern, food-grade milling, optical color sorting, consumer packaging, and high-purity pharmaceutical manufacturing in Punjab.

01 • GEOLOGICAL FOUNDATION

The Punjab Salt Range Mineral Base

Continuous evaporite seams of the ancient Salt Range Formation span northern Punjab across Jhelum, Khushab, Chakwal, and Mianwali districts. Proved accessible commercial reserves exceed 1.25 billion metric tonnes of high-purity crystalline halite.

02 • INDUSTRIAL SITES & INFRASTRUCTURE

Where to Build: Processing Zones & Logistics

Punjab offers structured industrial real estate for pink salt processing, ranging from mineral-adjacent clusters in Khushab to fully notified Special Economic Zones (SEZs) with dedicated utilities and port highway links.

Provincial Flagship Processing Zone

Quaidabad Pink Salt Mineral Processing Zone (Khushab)

Situated just 18 km from the high-purity Warcha salt seams, the 110-acre Quaidabad Zone is engineered as Punjab's central processing hub. It integrates raw stone receiving, optical grading, food-grade packaging, and container logistics linked directly to the M-2 Motorway and Karachi export ports.

03 • INDUSTRIAL VALUE ESCALATION

The 6-Stage Pink Salt Value-Addition Ladder

A single, unified progression detailing product specifications, indicative FOB pricing bands, capital requirements, and applicable quality standards from raw extraction to pharmaceutical API sodium chloride.

Strategic Rationale: Raw rock salt exported as unrefined bulk lumps yields only $35 to $50 per metric tonne. Investing in optical color sorting, stainless-steel food-grade milling (Codex CXS 150), and automated nitrogen-flush retail packaging elevates realization to $550–$950/MT (up to a 16x multiplier), while culinary tableware and cGMP pharmaceutical refining achieve substantial high-margin foreign exchange earnings.

06 • REGULATORY CONFORMANCE

Export Compliance & Standards Matrix

Technical compliance pathways under the Pakistan Single Window (PSW), destination market food-safety thresholds, tariff classifications, and official legislative documents.

Pakistan Single Window (PSW) Export Journey

1
Trader Profile Registration

Establish electronic NTN, STRN, chamber membership, and commercial bank authorized dealer code in PSW.

2
Laboratory Assay Certificate

Obtain pre-shipment heavy metals and purity analysis from ISO 17025 accredited labs (PAFDA, PCSIR, SGS, TTI).

3
Customs Goods Declaration

Electronic filing of GD under HS 2501 with commercial invoice, packing list, and SBP Form-E financial linkage.

4
Container Sealing & Bill of Lading

Dry container stuffing with ISPM-15 heat-treated pallets, customs seal inspection, and port terminal dispatch.
