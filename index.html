Section 1.A • Geological Intelligence

Pakistan's Pink Salt Resource Potential

Sources: GSP (2024) & PMDC Operations Register (2026)

Pakistan's Salt Range represents one of the world's most significant and ancient evaporite formations, originating in the Eocambrian era over 500 million years ago. Continuous subsurface seams span northern Punjab across Jhelum, Khushab, Chakwal, and Mianwali districts.

Rigorous Classification Note on Regional Figures:
While an authoritative regional resource estimate of over 30.0+ billion tonnes has historically been proposed for the wider Salt Range evaporite belt, responsible mineral economics requires clear scientific distinction between categories:
1. Geological Resources 30.0+ Billion MT (Indicative Resource)

Total estimated in-situ sedimentary evaporite mineralisation across the entire 300-km Salt Range strike, based on regional stratigraphic extrapolation.

2. Surveyed Reserves 10.0+ Billion MT (Surveyed Reserves)

Documented deposits confirmed through detailed borehole telemetry and mine development surveys by the Geological Survey of Pakistan (GSP, 2024).

3. Economically Recoverable 1.5+ Billion MT (Active Leases)

Directly accessible via established underground room-and-pillar mining operations at Khewra (600M+ MT) and Warcha (400M+ MT) under PMDC concession management.

Official Reference: Geological Survey of Pakistan (GSP) Salt Range Stratigraphic Memoir (Published: 2024) | Pakistan Mineral Development Corporation (PMDC) Concessions Register (Audited: January 2026).

Section 1.B • Mineral Composition & Compliance

Distinctive Characteristics & Scientific Integrity

Pakistani Himalayan Pink Salt is characterized by its unrefined crystalline structure and distinct aesthetic hue. Commercial and scientific assessment reveals:

Natural Chemical Composition

  • Sodium Chloride (NaCl): 98.8% to 99.2% dry basis purity across primary seams.
  • Naturally Occurring Trace Elements: Micro-concentrations of iron oxides (Fe₂O₃, 20–50 ppm), giving signature peach, rose, and ruby hues, alongside trace calcium (Ca), magnesium (Mg), and potassium (K).
  • Color Spectrum Variations: Transparent crystal white, pale pink, deep rose, and dark red—varying naturally across geological strata.
  • Multi-Industry Applications: Food-grade table seasonings, animal nutrition lick blocks, artisanal decor, and cosmetic spa formulations.

🛡️ Mandatory Regulatory & Scientific Disclaimer

No Unsupported Medical Claims: Claims that pink salt contains 5% of clinically beneficial minerals or that it treats disease, reduces hypertension, or physiologically detoxifies the body are not supported by clinical evidence and must not be published. Trace minerals impart distinct culinary flavor, aesthetic coloration, and textural properties, but the salt is predominantly sodium chloride and must comply with dietary sodium guidelines and Codex Alimentarius standards.

Section 1.D • Technical Quality Control

Quality Challenges and the Importance of Proper Processing

Raw mined salt cannot enter high-value consumer markets directly. Investors must recognize and design systems to overcome key industrial quality challenges:

Critical Quality Challenges:

  1. Inconsistent Raw Feed Quality: Seam-to-seam variations in color, hardness, and natural impurities.
  2. Mine Handling Contamination: Dust, transport silt, and floor contact during extraction and haulage.
  3. Blasting & Extraction Residues: Must be analytical assessed through accredited testing (blasting residues do not affect all salt, but must be verified).
  4. Inconsistent Particle Sizing: Irregular granules causing caking and customer dissatisfaction.
  5. Lack of Toxic Metal Screening: Failure to test for Lead, Arsenic, Cadmium, and Mercury limits.
  6. Inadequate Barrier Packaging: Moisture ingress causing recrystallization and hardness.

🚿 The Washing Process: Trade-Offs & Operational Costs

Washing is a conditional processing option, not universally mandatory. It is essential for food-grade retail salt with surface dirt or elevated insoluble matter, but unnecessary for decorative lamps, de-icing salt, or dry-screened animal licks.

Associated Washing Costs in Feasibility Model:
• Water consumption (~1.5 m³ per tonne @ PKR 180/m³)
• Effluent handling & settling ponds (~PKR 120/tonne)
• Post-wash drying energy (18 kWh/tonne thermal/electric)
• Specialized SS304 rotary trommel equipment (~PKR 14–28 Lakh)

Quality Assurance & Standards

Quality & Laboratory Testing Directory

Authoritative standards (Codex, ISO, PSQCA), analytical testing methods, accredited laboratory directory for Lahore & Pakistan, and testing cost calculator.

Relevant International & National Standards

Standard Title / Scope Key Requirements & Limits Relevance to Investor
Codex CXS 150-1985 Standard for Food Grade Salt Min 97.0% NaCl dry basis; Arsenic ≤ 0.5 mg/kg, Cadmium ≤ 0.5 mg/kg, Lead ≤ 2.0 mg/kg, Mercury ≤ 0.1 mg/kg, Copper ≤ 2.0 mg/kg Mandatory baseline for all food-grade exports to EU, US, and GCC retail.
Codex CXS 192-1995 General Standard for Food Additives Permissible anti-caking agents: Potassium ferrocyanide max 10 mg/kg (or strictly additive-free for organic certification). Required for consumer table salt packaging formulations.
Codex CXS 193-1995 Contaminants & Toxins in Food Strict maximum limits for environmental contaminants, heavy metals, and radionuclide screening. Required for customs clearance under foreign food inspection protocols.
ISO 17025:2017 Testing & Calibration Laboratories Competence requirements for chemical assay, traceability of measurement, and certified reference standards. All export test reports must originate from ISO 17025 accredited labs.
ISO 9297 / ISO 2483 Chemical Assay & Moisture Determination Argentometric titration for chloride (NaCl content); loss of mass at 110°C (moisture ≤ 0.5% dry basis). Standard test protocols executed on every export batch.
PSQCA 166:2008 Pakistan Edible Salt Standard Compulsory national certification mark for edible salt manufactured or packaged within Pakistan. Mandatory for domestic distribution and export licensing in Punjab.

ICP-OES Spectroscopy

Inductively Coupled Plasma Optical Emission

Simultaneously quantifies multi-element trace minerals (Iron, Calcium, Magnesium, Potassium) down to parts-per-million (ppm) levels to establish authentic geological provenance.

ICP-MS Ultra-Trace Analysis

Inductively Coupled Plasma Mass Spectrometry

High-sensitivity detection of toxic heavy metals (Lead, Arsenic, Cadmium, Mercury) down to parts-per-billion (ppb) levels. Required for US FDA and EU EFSA compliance.

AAS Elemental Assay

Atomic Absorption Spectroscopy

Cost-effective routine method for individual metal assays (e.g. Iron, Copper) suitable for high-frequency internal quality control and daily shift batch verification.
