## 8. "8x to 15x" Value-Addition Multiplier Basis

* **Underlying Rationale:** Unprocessed bulk rock salt extracted underground is sold at mine mouth for **$35 to $50 per MT** (Rs. 10 to 14 per kg).
* **Processed Benchmark:** Value-added consumer stand-up retail pouches achieve **$550 to $800+ per MT** FOB Karachi ($0.55 to $0.80 per kg), representing an **11x to 16x value multiplier**.
* **Artisanal & Gourmet Benchmark:** Gourmet glass grinders and infused specialty salts achieve **$2,000 to $3,500 per MT** ($2.00 to $3.50 per kg), representing a **40x to 70x value multiplier**.
* **Policy Recommendation:** Replace isolated slogan "8x–15x Value" with explicit comparative ladder: *"Elevating export revenue from $35–$50/MT for raw bulk rock up to $550–$950/MT for retail food-grade pouches and $2,000+/MT for gourmet tableware."*
