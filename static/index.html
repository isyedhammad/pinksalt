International Trade & Standards

Export Intelligence & Compliance

Customs procedures, Pakistan Single Window (PSW), tariff codes, and global food safety standards.

Pakistan Single Window (PSW) Export Journey

  1. Trader Profile Registration: Register corporate NTN and digital identity on the Pakistan Single Window portal (psw.gov.pk).
  2. Electronic E-Form Filing: Issue computerized financial E-Form via commercial authorized dealer bank for export proceeds tracking.
  3. Goods Declaration (GD): File electronic GD under HS Code 2501.00. Attach packing list, commercial invoice, and Bill of Lading.
  4. Mandatory Certifications: Obtain Certificate of Origin from TDAP and Phytosanitary Certificate for wooden pallets.

Key Destination Import Standards

United States (US FDA): 21 CFR compliance. Requires Foreign Supplier Verification Program (FSVP) and heavy metal compliance (Pb < 0.5 ppm).

European Union (EFSA): Regulation (EC) No 178/2002. Traceability from mine to distributor, food-grade contact materials.

Codex Alimentarius (CXS 150-1985): Global baseline: Minimum 97.0% NaCl, Cadmium < 0.5 ppm, Mercury < 0.1 ppm.

Gulf Standards (GSO): Halal certification and tamper-evident packaging.
