# PinkSalt Intelligence & Investment Portal: Sources, Demo Data & Verification Register

## 1. Context & Purpose
This platform was conceived and developed as a strategic concept proposal by an **Assistant Director in the Mines & Minerals Sector, Government of Punjab, Pakistan**, for presentation and stakeholder discussion at the **Pakistan Mineral Investment Forum**.

Its core mandate is to illustrate how digital technology, deterministic financial modeling, and grounded retrieval AI can transform raw geological and administrative data into actionable, bankable investment concepts.

## 3. Labeling of Demonstration & Conceptual Records

To maintain absolute institutional credibility and avoid misleading investors, the platform explicitly classifies data records into distinct tiers:

### A. Documented / Verified Records
- **Khewra Salt Mine Seam**: Documented reserves exceeding 600M tonnes, 98.8% average NaCl purity, managed by PMDC.
- **Warcha Salt Mine Seam**: High crystalline transparency, 99.1% average NaCl purity, managed by PMDC.
- **Kalabagh Evaporite Outcrops**: 250M+ tonnes estimated resource, 97.9% average purity.

### B. Demonstrative Project Opportunities & Conceptual Joint Ventures
- **Quaidabad Common Facility & Central Optical Sorting Center**: Conceptual public-private partnership (PPP) model designed to illustrate common processing infrastructure near Warcha.
- **Food-Grade Optical Sorting & Stand-Up Pouch Retail Plant (25 TPD)**: Calibrated industrial engineering model based on Buhler/Tomra sorting specifications and packaging economics.
- **Gourmet Ceramic-Mill Grinders Facility (15 TPD)**: Illustrative model based on European supermarket specifications and Export Development Fund (EDF) guidelines.
- **Pharmaceutical API Sodium Chloride Refinery (50 TPD)**: Technical concept based on cGMP dissolution and vacuum recrystallization standards.

### C. Simulated Traceability Batches
- `PK-SALT-2026-KHW-001`, `PK-SALT-2026-WCH-042`, `PK-SALT-2026-KLB-108`: Realistic simulation records showing how batch assay certificates (NaCl %, moisture, heavy metals <0.01 ppm) are verified against the GI registry.

## 4. Missing Official Datasets, Credentials & Pre-Launch Integrations

Before transitioning this concept portal into an officially authorized public service of the Government of Punjab, the following integrations and approvals are required:

1. **Official Ministerial Endorsement & Hosting**:
   - Migration from demonstration domain to official provincial government subdomain (e.g. `pinksalt.punjab.gov.pk`).
   - Punjab Information Technology Board (PITB) security and penetration audit clearance.

2. **Live Spatial GIS Data**:
   - Direct API connection with the Punjab Mines Cadastre Information System (MCIS) for real-time lease boundary shapefiles and block coordinates.
   - High-resolution satellite geological overlays from SUPARCO.

3. **Pakistan Single Window (PSW) Direct Integration**:
   - Production API credentials for PSW customs clearance to enable live HS Code export shipment tracking.

4. **Laboratory Management Information System (LIMS)**:
   - Direct digital integration with Punjab Mines Testing Laboratories and PCSIR (Pakistan Council of Scientific and Industrial Research) to auto-populate batch assay certificates.

5. **Production AI Model Credentials**:
   - Provisioning of dedicated enterprise Google Gemini API credentials (`GEMINI_API_KEY`) hosted within secure government cloud infrastructure.
